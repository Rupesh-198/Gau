A few words from my heart

For Gautami

I don't know where this goes, but I wanted you to know what I feel.
♡

Gautami, I texted you one day, and you were the one who replied to me. Slowly, our conversations started, and then they became deeper. We started having Instagram calls, then normal calls, then video calls. We slowly started meeting too. And now it feels like we barely go two or three days without meeting, and on all the other days, somehow, we end up seeing each other.

I honestly don't know what exactly happened between you and your ex, or why you both broke up. I don't even know whether it happened in February or May. I know very little about that part of your life. Suddenly, I came into your life, you started talking to me, and I don't know why you became so comfortable or attached to me. But slowly, I became involved in your life too. I got used to having you around. I don't know when I became attached to you or when feelings for you started developing in my heart. I just know that it happened without me even realizing it.

And Gautami, I understand that you cannot simply forget a relationship that lasted for so many years. Somewhere, there will always be some attachment. I don't know whether what you feel for him now is anger, love, or just attachment. But when you tell me certain stories about him, when you continue the bhishi money arrangement, when you still had to return his umbrella, and when you went to his aunt's place for his birthday, seeing all these things honestly makes me confused about what is actually going on in your heart.

And after what happened when you went to his aunt's place, I was genuinely shocked, Gautami.

Look, it's your life. I don't want to interfere in your life, and I know I don't have the right to tell you what you should or shouldn't do. I don't even want to control anything. But there is one thing that has been bothering me deeply. After you went there and everything that happened afterward, something inside me changed.

It feels like your past has suddenly come back into your life. Earlier, our texts may not have been as frequent, but we still had our calls. Then suddenly, there were calls because of the watch, then a video call, then a normal call again. And Gautami, honestly, all of this has made me feel like maybe I am just an option for you.

If you genuinely feel that you want to keep me in your life, I will never interfere with your decisions. But I don't want to be someone's option. That feeling hurts me.

It feels like your old priority is slowly coming back into your life, and somewhere in between all of this, I am slowly becoming invisible.

I'm not asking you to choose me over anyone. I'm not asking you to forget your past. I just want to understand where I actually stand in your life.

If I mean something to you, I want to know that. And if I mean nothing to you, then please be honest with me. I'll accept it and I'll still be happy for you.

I just don't want to stay somewhere in between—where I'm important when you need me, but invisible when your past comes back.

You don't have to answer immediately.
I just wanted you to know what has been going on in my heart.

— Rupesh
